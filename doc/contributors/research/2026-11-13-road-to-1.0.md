# The road to 1.0: what stands between Tokra and a port of Tauri

2026-11-13. Read-only inventory, made on branch `road-to-1` from `trunk`
at `7625e0d`. Nothing but this file was changed. No rake task, test,
build, example, or Docker run was made; every claim below comes from
reading files and from `git grep`, `grep`, and `ls`.

ADR 015's sections and line numbers below are those of ADR 015 at
`7625e0d`. Its sections 1 to 3 (the gem, `tokra dev`, `tokra build`) now
live in [the CLI's design](../design/cli.md), and sections 4 to 9 (what
ships, the packaged context, the `bundle` keys, platform specifics, the
Fledge pin, and testing) and its answered questions in
[packaging through Fledge](../design/packaging.md).

What it was read against:

- Tauri: the pinned submodule `vendor/tauri` at `9f0306fbc`
  (`crates/tauri` 2.9.5, locking wry 0.54.0, tao 0.34.5, muda 0.17.1,
  tray-icon 0.21.0). Paths below under `crates/` and `packages/` are in
  that submodule.
- Tauri's official plugins: a shallow clone of
  `https://github.com/tauri-apps/plugins-workspace` in the worktree's
  ignored `tmp/plugins-workspace`, at `main` = `9aa4f717` (2026-11-10),
  plus commit `d4613ff0` (2026-01-26), the commit Tokra's plugins were
  ported from (plans 021, 023, 024, 025). Plugin versions below are the
  `Cargo.toml` versions at `9aa4f717` unless a row says otherwise.
- Ferro: its repository (https://github.com/setdef/Ferro) as it stands,
  read only.

Every row is meant to be handed out alone. Each names the Tauri item and
where it lives, Tokra's state, the evidence, what it waits on, a size,
and whether it needs Rust in `ext/`. Sizes: **S** under a day for one
agent in a few files; **M** several files and real-window tests; **L** a
new subsystem. "Native" means new Rust in `ext/tokra/src/`; "Ruby" means
`lib/` or a plugin alone; "Ferro" means it needs a Ferro gem that does
not exist yet (the former ADR 011 (now ADR 020)).

States: **ported**; **partly** (with what is missing); **not ported**;
**declined** (deliberately not ported, with the recorded reason); **n/a**
(no Ruby meaning, with the reason).

## Summary

Tokra is a credible port of Tauri's windowing core and not yet of the
rest. What a Ruby developer can do from Ruby, it does much as Tauri
does: windows, the webview window builder, menus, trays, events, IPC and
channels, resources, images, state, restart and exit, and the config's
schema. What a Tauri app is made of beyond that is mostly missing: its
frontend served from `frontendDist`, its security model, its JavaScript
APIs for windows and paths, most of its plugins, its CLI, its bundles,
and its updater.

Counted by the rows below (each row a module, type, key, command group,
or plugin; not weighted by size):

| Area | Rows | Ported | Partly | Not ported | Declined or n/a |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Core runtime | 48 | 15 | 13 | 17 | 3 |
| 2. Config keys | 20 | 3 | 5 | 11 (10 read and ignored) | 1 |
| 3. Security | 11 | 1 | 1 | 8 | 1 |
| 4. Plugins (26 with desktop support) | 26 | 4 | 1 | 21 | (5 mobile-only, n/a) |

By `@tauri-apps/api` module, 7 of 12 work (`core` but for
`convertFileSrc`, `dpi`, `event`, `image`, `menu`, `mocks`, `tray`), and
5 do not (`app`, `path`, `webview`, `webviewWindow`, `window`). Of the
four ported plugins, all follow the January 2026 upstream commit, and the
log plugin answers no command from the page.

What most blocks calling it a port, in order:

1. **No capabilities, permissions, ACL, or scopes** (S-01 to S-04,
   S-11). Every command is open to the app's own pages and closed to all
   others, and every plugin that needs a scope (fs, shell, http, opener,
   the asset protocol) has nothing to stand on.
2. **The frontend is not served from `frontendDist` or `devUrl`** (C-11,
   C-38, CF-15, CF-16). A Tauri app's ordinary shape, a folder of built
   files or a dev server, does not load; pages come from a Rack app or a
   hand-registered `tokra` handler. Development versus packaged is an
   open question (C-13; question 7).
3. **The page cannot drive windows, webviews, the app, or paths** (J-06
   to J-10; plan 050c, waiting on question 1). A page that handles
   `onCloseRequested` cannot close its own window.
4. **No `tokra dev`, `tokra build`, bundles, or updater** (CLI-01 to
   CLI-03, B rows, U rows). ADR 015, still Proposed, is the design.
5. **Most plugins are missing**, including the everyday ones: fs, shell,
   opener, os, process, store, http, window-state, deep-link,
   single-instance (P-06 to P-26).
6. **The path resolver answers one directory** (C-42), which store,
   window-state, fs, and log's file targets all need.
7. **Plugins cannot be configured or registered the Tauri way**:
   no `Builder::plugin`, no plugin `Builder`, no `config.plugins`
   hand-off, no plugin global scripts (C-02, C-37, C-41, CF-20).

## How deep this went

- **Core runtime.** Module and type level for every public module of
  `crates/tauri/src` (`lib.rs:67-311` re-exports). Method level for
  `App`, `AppHandle`, `Builder`, `Manager`, `Window`, `Webview`,
  `WebviewWindowBuilder`, `Plugin`, `RunEvent`, and the core plugins'
  command lists, by comparing Tauri's `pub fn` lists with Tokra's `def`
  lists. `Window` was diffed mechanically; its builder rows were taken
  from `lib/tokra/webview_window_builder.rb`'s method list. `Error`'s
  variants were not audited one by one.
- **Config.** Key level, from plan 054's inventory, checked against
  `git grep` of `lib/` for each key's reader outside
  `lib/tokra/utils/config/`. The `bundle` section's nested keys were not
  re-audited: plan 054 phase 4 and ADR 015 §6 cover them.
- **Security.** Module level (`tauri-utils/src/acl/`, `ipc/authority.rs`,
  `scope/`, `protocol/isolation.rs`, `crates/tauri/permissions/`).
- **Plugins.** Command level, from each plugin's `build.rs` `COMMANDS`
  list at `9aa4f717`, for the plugins Tokra ports; plugin level for the
  rest, with their crate dependencies from `Cargo.toml`. The ported
  plugins' option structs and guest-js were not re-audited.
- **CLI and bundling.** Command level, from `tauri-cli/src/lib.rs`
  `enum Commands` and each subcommand's enum; bundle formats from
  `tauri-bundler/src/bundle/`. Tokra's side is ADR 015 as it stands on
  trunk (Proposed, and being revised by another agent).
- **JavaScript.** Module level for `packages/api/src`, and per plugin npm
  package, judged by which `plugin:NAME|command` invokes Tokra answers.
  No page was run.

## 9. Questions for the maintainer

### Asked in the records and still open

1. **Which window and webview commands may a page call before
   capabilities exist?** (a) Tauri's default sets only, so
   `onCloseRequested`'s `destroy` rejects; (b) every command for the
   app's own pages; (c) the default sets plus a code-level opt-in per
   capability. Plan 050, question 1 (`plan/050.md:970`). 050c waits on
   it, and with it J-06 to J-08 and S-11. This is the "window and
   webview plugin permissions until an ACL exists (050c)" lead.
2. **What stands for `debug_assertions` and the `devtools` feature?** A
   `Builder` opt-in named after the feature, or never. Plan 050, question
   2 (`plan/050.md:989`). Devtools (C-28, C-30, CF-04) and
   `tokra build --debug` (ADR 015 §3) wait on it.
3. **Should a webview window's webview become a child, as under Tauri's
   `unstable`?** Options (a), (b), (c). Plan 052, question 1
   (`plan/052.md:3060`). Phase 4 (052d: C-26, C-29, `reparent`,
   `set_auto_resize`) and plan 050's phase 3 `reparent` wait on it.
4. **Does Tokra build in `macos-private-api`?** (a) build it in and gate
   it on `app.macOSPrivateApi`; (b) leave it out of the shipped gem; (c)
   never. Plan 052, question 2 (`plan/052.md:3089`). Until then
   `transparent: true` is ignored on macOS (CF-04, CF-06), and the HTML
   fullscreen API stays off.
5. **Does Tokra build in wry's `mac-proxy`?** (a) raise the macOS floor
   to 14; (b) weak-link and refuse below 14; (c) leave `proxy_url` inert
   on macOS, as now. Plan 052, question 3 (`plan/052.md:3117`).
6. **Does Tokra keep Tauri's Linux incognito gap?** (a) keep it, as
   052e-3 shipped; (b) deviate. Plan 052, question 4
   (`plan/052.md:3136`).
7. **How does an app tell development from packaged?** Plan 054,
   question 1 (`plan/054.md:2104`), recommends (c), packaged by default
   with a `dev:` opt-in; ADR 015 §5 (Proposed) answers with (a),
   development unless `tokra build` made it, sealed by signing. The two
   records disagree until ADR 015 is accepted. 054a-5 (C-13, CF-10,
   CF-15, S-09) waits on it.
8. **Are tomlrb's differences from toml 0.9.10 acceptable for
   `Tokra.toml`?** Plan 054, question 2 (`plan/054.md:2139`). The TOML
   half of phase 2 (CF-01) waits on it. This is the "Plan 054 Q2" lead.
9. **May Tokra refuse an invalid GTK app id where Tauri does not?** Plan
   054, question 3 (`plan/054.md:2155`). Phase 4 shipped the refusal
   (CHANGELOG lines 517-519), so this asks for approval after the fact.
10. **Does a gem-distributed app get the packaged marker?** ADR 015,
    question 1 (`adr/015.md:552`).
11. **Defaults that ask for more than Fledge has** (`targets: "all"`, and
    refusing a Windows installer with `webviewInstallMode` unset until
    Fledge's B1). ADR 015, question 2 (`adr/015.md:566`).
12. **Is ADR 015 accepted?** Its status is "Proposed" (`adr/015.md:9-15`)
    and another agent is revising it; every CLI and B row waits on it.
13. **When to publish.** Plan 059 (`plan/059.md:17-24`) is open, waiting
    on Ferro's plan 011, which lists six decisions waiting on the
    maintainer (`Ferro/doc/contributors/plan/011.md:46`). Nothing here is
    installable from RubyGems.org until then (README.md "Installation").

## 10. What this note could not determine, and why

- **Whether the ported plugins match their current upstream versions.**
  Tokra ported `d4613ff0`; this note compared only command lists against
  `9aa4f717`. Option structs, guest-js payloads, and error texts were not
  diffed. Dialog's `message` against the newer guest-js is the likeliest
  break.
- **Whether every `Window` method behaves as Tauri's.** Presence was
  checked by name; behavior was not. Plans 040, 041, and 045 hold the
  audits.
- **`Error`'s variants.** `crates/tauri/src/error.rs` was not compared
  variant by variant with `lib/tokra/error.rb`.
- **What a page sees for a `tokra://` request no handler answers.**
  `dispatch_http_request` (`lib/tokra/app.rb:662-686`) sends nothing in
  that case; whether wry then times out, hangs, or errors was not run.
- **Fledge's actual state.** The B rows trust ADR 015's account of Fledge
  0.5.x and its capability ids; Fledge's repository was not
  read.
- **ADR 015's revision.** Another agent is revising it now; the CLI and
  B rows describe trunk's copy.
- **The plugins' version at `9aa4f717` versus the latest npm release.**
  The shallow clone is `main`, not a tag; a released package may differ.
- **Runtime behavior of anything.** No test, example, or app was run, by
  the rules of this task.
